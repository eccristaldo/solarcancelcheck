Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Solar Cancel Check ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit solarcancelcheck.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website or submit a request, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, email address, and phone number (if provided)
  • Information about your solar contract or situation
  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Cookies and usage data (pages visited, time spent, clicks)
  • Referring URLs and advertising click identifiers (e.g., Google Click ID)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Determine your eligibility for solar contract cancellation assistance
  • Connect you with qualified legal review professionals
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Measure and improve our advertising campaigns
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Legal review partners: Third-party professionals who help evaluate your case
  • Advertising platforms: Such as Google Ads, for conversion tracking purposes
  • Analytics providers: To understand how our site is used
  • Affiliate networks: For tracking and reporting purposes

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and session information
  • Track advertising performance and measure conversions
  • Analyze traffic and improve our website

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

5. Google Ads & Conversion Tracking

We use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. This may involve the use of Google Click IDs (gclid) passed through our website to our advertising partners. This data is used solely for measuring advertising performance and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
